# Motochain Service design direction

Direction selected by the agent at the user's request. Anti Slop applies during development.

The product borrows the readability of a workshop service booklet: warm paper, charcoal type, rust-red primary actions, and green reserved for confirmed records. This connects the interface to maintenance history rather than cryptocurrency speculation.

Use a text-only product wordmark. No invented logo, testimonials, usage metrics, or workshop identities. Example motorcycles and maintenance entries are explicitly demonstration data.

Use Segoe UI / system sans for familiar Indonesian form text, with Georgia for editorial headings reminiscent of a printed booklet. Dates and mileage align in a ledger. Solid dividers organize records; no decorative grid, gradients, floating cards, or looping animations.

Navbar separates the garage from service entry. The garage presents motorcycles as selectable entries. Motorcycle information contains its service timeline; a separate service-entry page contains the selector, receipt assistant and reviewable form. Detail and entry pages use a narrow selector and broad working area on desktop; mobile stacks them. Public visitors can read history. A light fixed theme serves public inspection in daylight and a printed-service-book identity. Focus uses a visible high-contrast outline. Actions are at least 44px high.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1. Short hover/focus transitions only. Status labels communicate state using words and color together. Empty, loading, denied, unavailable, and pending-transaction states explain the next step.
